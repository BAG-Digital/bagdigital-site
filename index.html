COMMON QUESTIONS

Plain-English answers.

What does BAGDigital actually do?

It connects business software so information can move between systems and repetitive operational work can be reduced. The goal is fewer manual handoffs and better answers without replacing everything you use.

Do I have to replace QuickBooks, my CRM, or my email?

No. The main idea is the opposite: keep the systems that already work for your business and connect them where useful.

Can BAGDigital connect to any software?

Not every product can be connected. It depends on whether the software supports a secure connection and whether the right access is available for your account.

Can I use BAGDigital today?

BAGDigital is being introduced gradually. Tell us which software you use and what work takes too much time, and we can tell you whether your business is a fit for the integrations currently available.

Where does AI fit in?

AI can make it easier to ask questions, summarize information, and prepare routine work. BAGDigital helps connect the right business information while keeping access and actions controlled.

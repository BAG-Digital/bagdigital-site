01 / THE PROBLEM

Software isn’t the problem. Coordination is.

Businesses often own enough software already. The expensive part is the human effort required to keep every system, inbox, calendar, document, and job state aligned.

01

Facts are scattered.

Customer status lives in one place. Email context lives somewhere else. Scheduling, files, estimates, and supplier details drift apart.

02

People become the API.

Owners and operations teams copy, search, re-enter, remember, and reconcile information that their software should already be helping them understand.

03

Work stalls invisibly.

A job is “open,” but the real question is: what is blocked, why is it blocked, what evidence proves it, and what should safely happen next?

02 / THE MODEL

One controlled layer between intent and execution.

BAGDigital Core is being built so products and AI interfaces do not need unrestricted, direct access to every provider. They ask for approved capabilities through a tenant-bound, auditable boundary.

Available · foundation

Connect without rip-and-replace.

Keep the systems your business already depends on. Core provides reusable tenant, workspace, connection, and persistence foundations around them.

Development

Keep credentials controlled.

The active M3 milestone is the tenant credential vault: server-side secret lifecycle, tenant-bound resolution, rotation, revocation, and auditable access without putting keys into business records.

Roadmap

Turn state into safe action.

Capability discovery, events, webhooks, a provider-neutral business graph, authorized read APIs, and repeatable onboarding build toward controlled automation.

Operating principle Read before write. Draft before execute. Approve before automate.

05 / SECURITY

AI does not get a skeleton key.

Security is part of the product architecture, not a cleanup pass after the demo works. Core is designed to keep tenant scope, credentials, capabilities, and approval boundaries explicit.

T

Tenant isolation

Durable state is scoped by organization and workspace, with database-level row security as an enforcement boundary rather than application filters alone.

K

Credentials stay controlled

Provider secrets belong in a server-side tenant vault, not in customer tables, browser code, URLs, logs, or AI context. That vault is the active M3 milestone.

P

Least privilege

A connection should expose only the capabilities the tenant and provider permit. Missing or mismatched scope should fail closed.

A

Approval is real

BAGDigital’s current phase is read-first. Future provider writes must be explicitly authorized, validated, auditable, and separately gated.

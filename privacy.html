Privacy Notice

Effective: October 6, 2026

This notice explains what the BAGDigital public website collects when you contact us and how inquiry information is handled.

Information you choose to send

The inquiry form may collect your name, business name, email address, the general problem you want help with, and the description you submit. You can also contact BAGDigital directly by email instead of using the form.

Temporary form processor

The public inquiry form currently uses FormSubmit as a third-party relay. FormSubmit receives the form fields you submit and delivers the inquiry to BAGDigital by email. If you do not want to use that relay, use the direct email option on the contact section instead.

Do not send sensitive information

The public website is not a credential or customer-record intake channel. Do not submit passwords, API keys, access tokens, payment information, customer records, confidential job files, or other sensitive business data through the public form.

How BAGDigital uses inquiry information

BAGDigital uses inquiry information to review your request, respond to you, determine whether there may be a fit, maintain reasonable business records, and address abuse or security concerns. Submitting the form does not subscribe you to a mailing list.

Tracking and hosting

BAGDigital does not currently add advertising or analytics trackers to this public site. The site is hosted using GitHub Pages, and hosting or network providers may process ordinary technical request data as part of delivering the site.

Retention and requests

Inquiry information may be retained as reasonably needed to respond, maintain business records, handle security or abuse, or meet applicable legal obligations. You may contact BAGDigital about information you submitted.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to jaime@bagdigital.tech.

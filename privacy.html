Inquiry Privacy Notice

This source preview has its online inquiry form disabled. You may contact BAGDigital directly by email. The notice below describes that direct-contact option; any future working online form will receive updated provider and processing disclosures before release. This is not a description of customer account or platform data.

What information you provide

If you email BAGDigital directly, your email address and whatever information you choose to send will be received as business correspondence. The inactive on-page controls do not send or store the text shown in this source preview. Please do not send passwords, API keys, payment details, customer records, health information or other sensitive information.

How your inquiry is delivered

The protected on-page inquiry service is not accepting submissions in this preview. You may send a message from your own email application to jaime@bagdigital.tech. Your email provider and BAGDigital's business email provider process and transmit that email. Emailing us does not create a BAGDigital account.

How we use inquiries

BAGDigital uses inquiry information to understand your request, evaluate whether we can help, discuss a possible project and respond to you. Sending an email does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list. If you decide to work with BAGDigital, we will discuss the scope and costs separately before paid work begins.

Retention and other providers

Messages sent directly by email may be processed by the sender's email provider and BAGDigital's email provider. BAGDigital may retain inquiries received as business correspondence; we do not promise a specific deletion deadline for those email copies. Before an independently reviewed protected online form is activated, this notice must be updated with the actual CAPTCHA and email transport providers and their relevant handling/retention details.

Contact and requests

For questions about an inquiry, or to request that we review, correct or delete information you sent, email jaime@bagdigital.tech. We will review the request in light of our records and applicable obligations; a request is not a promise of immediate or complete deletion from email systems or backups. The on-page inquiry form is disabled in this source preview.

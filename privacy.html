Inquiry Privacy Notice

This notice explains how inquiries through the BAGDigital website are delivered and how we handle the information you provide. It applies to our public inquiry form when published; it does not describe customer account or platform data.

What information you provide

When you send an inquiry through our website, we ask for your name, email address, the service you're interested in, and a short description of your project. You can optionally provide your business name. The form also asks you to acknowledge how your inquiry is handled. Please do not send passwords, API keys, payment details, customer records, health information or other sensitive information.

How your inquiry is delivered

Our website uses FormSubmit.co to receive inquiry submissions and forward them to BAGDigital's business email at jaime@bagdigital.tech. Information you enter in the form is sent to FormSubmit.co when you select “Send my inquiry.” FormSubmit may display a verification or confirmation step. Submitting the form does not create a BAGDigital account.

How we use inquiries

BAGDigital uses inquiry information to understand your request, evaluate whether we can help, discuss a possible project and respond to you. Submitting this form does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list. If you decide to work with BAGDigital, we will discuss the scope and costs separately before paid work begins.

Retention and other providers

FormSubmit states in its documentation (opens in a new tab) that it retains form submissions in its archive for 30 days. That is the provider's published description, not a BAGDigital guarantee about deletion or backups. Read FormSubmit's privacy terms (opens in a new tab) for more information about its practices. BAGDigital may retain inquiries received by email as business correspondence; we do not promise a specific deletion deadline for those email copies.

Contact and requests

For questions about an inquiry, or to request that we review, correct or delete information you sent, email jaime@bagdigital.tech. We will review the request in light of our records and applicable obligations; a request is not a promise of immediate or complete deletion from FormSubmit or email systems. You may also contact us directly at that address instead of using the website form.
